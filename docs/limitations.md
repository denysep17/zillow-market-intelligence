# Limitations

- Public Zillow Research data is aggregate market data, not Zillow internal event-level telemetry.
- Predictive relationships do not imply causality.
- Housing indices may be revised or smoothed.
- Publication lag can reduce real-time usefulness.
- Metro-level averages hide within-market heterogeneity.
- Structural breaks can invalidate relationships learned from older periods.
- Some metrics may have inconsistent historical coverage across metros.

These limitations should be tested, quantified where possible, and surfaced in the final dashboard and model card.
