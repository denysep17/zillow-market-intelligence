# Metric dictionary

| Metric | Definition | Grain | Frequency | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ZHVI | Zillow Home Value Index | Metro | Monthly | Core outcome / momentum |
| ZORI | Zillow Observed Rent Index | Metro | Monthly | Demand / rent signal |
| Inventory | Available housing inventory | Metro | Monthly | Supply signal |
| New listings | Newly listed homes | Metro | Monthly | Supply flow |
| Price-cut share | Share of listings with price reductions | Metro | Monthly | Seller pressure |
| Days to pending | Time from listing to pending status | Metro | Monthly | Market velocity |
| Sale-to-list ratio | Sale price relative to list price | Metro | Monthly | Pricing pressure |

Definitions must be reconciled against the exact Zillow Research source methodology before modeling.
